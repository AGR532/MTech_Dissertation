# Steer By Wire Simulation
STEER FEEL MODELING OF STEER BY WIRE SYSTEM AND ITS SAFETY FACTORS AND IMPLICATIONS

X-by-wire technologies is a well innovative research area in the field of automation. This research project is intended to study the modelling and simulation of the Steer Feel module of the SbW system and the broad area comprises of the following
● Modelling and simulation: MATLAB to be used to Model and simulate the feedback
and control of the Steer Feel
● Risk Assessment: Assessment of risk factor and redundant setup.
X-by Wire is used in Active Safety application, such as Brake-By- Wire and Steer-By Wire and also used in certain applications in the power train such as Throttle-by-Wire. Though the system is much dependable, the haptic feedback from the mechanical counterpart is required to give us a much better user comfort and has a significant safety factor included in it. The dissertation is focused mainly on the Steer Feel Module based modelling and simulation. Scope of this dissertation is to model the Steer Feel function of the SbW and Risk assessment and impact of the Steer Feel on the safety of the whole SbW System.
